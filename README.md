### My model

**Long-Term Value** = **Valuable Offering** × **Efficient Delivery** × **Growth** × **Resilience** × **Adaptability**

The pillars multiply. If one is zero, the business is worth nothing.
Every decision I make strengthens at least one pillar without weakening another.

### How I build each pillar

#### 1. Valuable offering: build the right thing
- I start from the business problem, not the technology.
- I turn vague needs into clear requirements. Building the wrong thing is the most expensive mistake in software.

#### 2. Efficient delivery: build it lean
- I shape the smallest solution that creates the value.
- I avoid adding complexity that has to be paid for later.
- I guide AI through implementation. AI brings the speed; I set the constraints, review the output and hold the quality bar.

#### 3. Growth: ship faster and scale cheaply
- I measure systems so the next steps are not a guess.
- I automate repetitive human work so a job done once pays interest.

#### 4. Resilience: reduce risk
- I build isolated, testable modules where errors have boundaries.
- I avoid single points of failure, in both systems and knowledge.
- I take on dependencies deliberately, only when they pay for themselves.

#### 5. Adaptability: stay cheap to change
- I design composable architecture: small, single-purpose parts that combine the UNIX way.
- Parts can be replaced, recombined and extended, so changing direction doesn't mean a rewrite.
- Systems stay maintainable for years.

## My principles

- **Write down the problem**. Nothing gets built until the problem is defined.
- **Long-term value first.** Technology is a means, not the goal.
- **Composition is the key**. Build from small, focused parts with clear boundaries.
- **Start simple, then evolve.** A complex system that works always grows out of a simple one that worked.
- **Go deep**. Hide massive complexity behind a simple interface.
- **Text is the universal interface.** Anything that speaks text can be automated, by humans and AI alike.
- **Measure, don't guess.** Profile first, optimize what matters.
- **Keep it lean.** Smaller scope and fewer dependencies usually means fewer failures.
- **Keep it robust.** Efficiency without resilience is fragile.
